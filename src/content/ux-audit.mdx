I run hands-on UX audits that show software teams what's getting in their users' way, what to keep, and how to fix it, from developer tools and infrastructure software to consumer apps.

I understand both sides. On yours: maybe your product is already out there but doesn't quite work for the people using it, or you're under pressure to ship a feature and have doubts about it. On your users' side, I can speak for the people I've designed for, from online shoppers and travelers to database administrators, site reliability engineers and platform engineers, and the terms and workflows they expect.

I've spent my career designing interfaces and design systems, from consumer e-commerce and travel products to database observability tools, where I also worked directly in the front-end code with engineering teams. Some of that work is in <a href={withBase('/')}>my portfolio</a>.

## The service: hands-on UX audit

A UX audit is a hands-on expert review of your product, seen through your users' eyes. You get a report of what works and should be kept, and what gets in the way and should change, written so your team can turn it into tickets right away.

### When is it a good time for a UX audit?

- When support requests or user complaints keep pointing at the interface
- When a feature isn't being adopted and you're not sure why
- When your product grew feature by feature and no longer feels coherent
- When your design team is stretched or wants a fresh, outside view

### Why not just ask an AI?

You can, and you'll get a long list of plausible issues. I use AI too, to work faster. What you pay for is what AI cannot do:

- **I actually use your product.** I install it, set it up and run real workflows with real data, not just review screenshots.
- **I know your users.** I can tell what they find normal from what really slows them down.
- **I choose what matters.** You get fewer findings, ordered by urgency and impact, so your team doesn't waste time on the wrong ones.
- **I tell you what to keep.** That way it isn't "fixed" by accident.
- **I stand behind it.** We go through it together, I answer your team's questions, and I can make the fixes myself as an option.

## What you get

You get one written report, as long as your product needs and no longer. Its findings are split into two groups:

- **Keep:** what works well and why, so it survives future changes.
- **Change:** what gets in your users' way, why it matters, and what to do about it.

Where it helps, each finding includes a screenshot or screen recording that shows the issue. The report is plain, structured text with clear sections, easy for people to read and for AI tools to turn into tickets in Jira, GitHub or Linear. It comes as a zip folder of markdown files with the screenshots and recordings linked alongside, ready to open in Obsidian, VS Code or any AI tool.

## How it works

1. **Intro call (free, 30 min).** You tell me about your product, your users and what worries you. We pick the package.
2. **Access.** You give me access to the product, a test account or a self-hosted setup, and any docs your users rely on. The environment should be working by the kickoff; setting up complex self-hosted installs is quoted separately.
3. **Kickoff.** We agree on the flows to audit and who your main users are.
4. **Audit.** I use the product as your users would and write the report.
5. **Walkthrough.** We go through the report together on a short call, and I answer your team's questions.
